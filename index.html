03 — questions

Before you build an empire.

What is markets?+

A stock market for the city, built on Solana. You trade tokenized stocks and ETFs straight from your own wallet, and every trade settles on-chain in seconds.

Are these real stocks?+

They are xStocks: tokens issued by Backed that are backed 1:1 by the real shares, held with a regulated custodian, and track the share price. They are not the shares themselves and carry no voting rights. xStocks are not offered to US persons or in some other countries, so check that they are allowed where you live.

Who holds my money?+

You do. Every trade is a Jupiter swap that you sign in your own wallet. markets never takes custody, never sees your keys and cannot move your funds.

What does a trade cost?+

The Solana network fee (a fraction of a cent) and the price impact of the route, both shown before you sign. markets adds no fee of its own.

Why does a stock say “thin market”?+

Its on-chain pool holds less than $5,000, so its price can be off and a trade would move it a lot. We show it honestly instead of hiding it, and the swap screen tells you the exact impact before you sign.

Is markets part of a game or a brokerage?+

No. markets is independent. It is not affiliated with any game studio, with Backed, with Jupiter, or with the companies whose stocks are listed. Nothing here is financial advice.
