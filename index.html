06 — Questions

Before you trade.

What is Bukz?
A trading terminal for the city: tokenized stocks, perps, fresh pump.fun coins and 24 city assets that each become a coin, all traded from your own Solana wallet. $BUKZ is the city's coin.
Who holds my money?
You do. Bukz builds the transaction, your wallet shows it to you and signs it. Nothing is ever deposited with us.
Are these real stocks?
They are xStocks: tokens issued by Backed, each backed one to one by the real share held with a regulated custodian, with a price that tracks the share. They are not the shares themselves and carry no voting rights. xStocks are not offered to US persons or in some other countries.
What does it cost?
Bukz adds no fee to trades. You pay the Solana network fee and the price impact of the route, both shown before you sign. Perps pay Jupiter's open, close and borrow fees. A city launch costs what pump.fun charges to create a coin, plus your optional first buy.
What is the trenches tab?
A live feed of pump.fun coins: just launched, close to graduating, and just graduated, with holders, top-10 share, dev share and snipers from Jupiter's data. Any of them buys or sells through Jupiter in one click. These coins are extremely risky and most of them go to zero.
What is a city coin?
Each of the 24 city assets becomes a real coin on pump.fun the first time someone claims it. The founder earns pump.fun's creator fees on every trade of it. It is a memecoin named after a city asset, not a share of a real building, business or vehicle, and it can go to zero.
How risky are perps?
Very. With leverage a small move against you is a large loss, and if the price reaches your liquidation price you lose your whole collateral. Borrow fees accrue while a position is open.
Is Bukz part of any company listed here?
No. Bukz is independent and is not affiliated with Backed, Jupiter, pump.fun or any listed company. Nothing here is financial advice.
